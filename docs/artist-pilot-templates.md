# Artist pilot forms and unsent drafts

Companion to [the pilot runbook](artist-pilot-kit.md). Copy the blank forms into
a private study folder before filling them. Do not commit completed participant
forms, recordings, private URLs or contact details to this repository.

All bracketed fields need a real value before use. No recipient is enrolled,
contacted, quoted or represented by these examples.

## Facilitator script

Opening: "We're testing the software, not your skills. Please work as you normally
would and say what you expect to happen. You can use the help built into the
product. I may stay quiet so I can see where the instructions are unclear. You
can skip a task or stop, and recording is off unless you chose otherwise."

Baseline questions:

1. Show or describe the last exhibition or portfolio update you made. What did
   you do yourself, and what did someone else do?
2. What took the most time? What did you pay for, if you are comfortable saying?
3. How do visitors currently ask about a work or reach your shop?
4. What would you need to keep if you changed website providers tomorrow?

During a task, neutral prompts are: "What would you try next?", "What do you
expect that to do?", and "Tell me what happened." Do not say "click the blue
button" or explain the intended answer. If the artist asks for help, ask whether
they want to continue independently or receive help; log the choice and actual
help. Stop immediately if private credentials or unrelated private content would
be exposed, and resume only after the participant has handled that privately.

Debrief:

1. What would make you choose your current website or another gallery tool?
2. Did the other-artist link help your exhibition, or distract from it? Why?
3. Which task, if any, would you ask someone else to do next month?
4. Would you use this for a second exhibition? What would have to change first?
5. What should be removed? What is still unclear about who controls the data?
6. Would you like a follow-up about a specific service, another research session,
   or neither? Confirm the route and time window; do not treat a yes as a sale.
