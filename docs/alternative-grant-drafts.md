## FUTO: an artist can leave the original host

**Proposed amount:** $3,000. **Work package:** FU-P1.
**Duration:** six weeks after a separately approved start.
**Funding relationship:** alternative funding for a slice already present in the
OpenSats scope. It is not additive funding for the same work.

### Reusable project text

Relay Gate is an MIT-licensed project for artist-controlled galleries. The useful
test of portability is whether an artist can take their reviewed catalog and
permitted media to another host without losing titles, sizes, descriptions or
links. A download button by itself does not establish that result.

This proposed six-week effort would verify and improve that exit path. Starting
from the existing reviewed build, it would document the export contract, test
restoration to a second clean environment, and record failures when the original
host is unavailable. An accessible list view would remain available when the
3D engine cannot run. No account with a central gallery service would be
required to read or reuse the export.

The public outputs would be synthetic fixtures, reproducible recovery
instructions, fixes for demonstrated failures, and a redacted usability report.
The scope excludes a hosted subscription service, marketing launch and payment
processing. Existing code would be cited as prior work, with only the future
verification and repairs included in the requested effort.

## BFTA: connected paintings and a practical workshop

**Working title:** Artist-owned routes: a small connected exhibition.
**Proposed USD planning equivalent:** $1,800. **Work package:** BA-A1.
**Duration:** eight weeks after approval and confirmed delivery arrangements.
**Funding relationship:** a separate artistic/educational project, outside the
software-development request. A venue, audience and artists are not yet agreed.

### Project summary draft

Artist-owned routes would be a small curated exhibition of paintings paired
with a practical workshop for artists and visitors. The artistic theme is how
artists describe, connect and sustain their work while keeping control of the
story. Each participating artist would choose a few paintings, approve their
images and interpretive text, and explain why a visitor might move from their
work to another artist's work. The connections would be editorial choices made
by artists, rather than paid ranking.

The proposed public presentation would serve **[specific U.S. audience/community
and confirmed venue or delivery channel]**. That detail must be supplied before
submission; general internet availability does not establish this project's
U.S. public benefit. A simple list/gallery would remain available for visitors
unable to use 3D. Physical labels and a short handout would connect the works to
their artist-approved descriptions.

The workshop would use the artworks to explain artist-controlled publishing,
portable files and direct Bitcoin support. Visitors could participate without
buying Bitcoin, installing a wallet or joining Nostr. Any payment demonstration
would use a synthetic example unless a separate, explicit payment test were
approved. Participation would not require artists to give up image rights or
permit recording of their voice.

The requested support would cover defined artist, curation, access and
presentation costs. Outcomes would include the agreed exhibition, a workshop,
an openly reusable instructional handout and an aggregate report of attendance,
questions and costs. Images and artist statements would use their own agreed
permissions; an open software license would not silently relicense the artworks.
No participating artist, venue, audience size or completed result is claimed in
this draft.

minimum useful next material is a consenting partner's problem statement,
threat model, safe reporting approach and explanation of why existing publishing
or payment tools are insufficient. Do not put partner identities or threat
details in a public repo. Review grant versus investment terms before deciding
on the funding route.

**NLnet:** collect truthful development provenance and evidence of human review,
identify a specific eligible fund and its European-dimension basis, and obtain
an owner decision about a permitted inquiry if ambiguity remains. Do not submit
this AI-assisted text as an undisclosed human proposal or treat the deadline as
a reason to conceal missing records.

**Creative Capital:** develop a distinct artistic concept and portfolio only
when TJ wants to pursue an individual artwork grant; wait for a verified next
call and applicant criteria. A product backlog is not the artistic proposal.

**Spiral and Geyser:** verify an actual live funding route, its terms, named
decision owner and current eligibility before adding any request amount or
deadline. No unsolicited outreach or public crowdfunding campaign is part of
this pack.
